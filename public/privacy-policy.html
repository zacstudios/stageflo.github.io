StageFlo Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Overview

StageFlo is a desktop worship presentation application developed by Zac Studios LTD. This Privacy Policy explains what information StageFlo accesses, stores, and transmits.

Information StageFlo Uses

StageFlo may access information that you create or import, including:

  • Songs, custom slides, media references, and Bible-related content
  • Service plans, presentation settings, and output configuration
  • Optional local app logs used for troubleshooting

By default, this information is stored locally on your device.

Data Collection and Transmission

StageFlo does not require account sign-in and does not sell personal information. The app may connect to network services only for product features that you enable, such as software update checks or optional remote/control related workflows.

Remote and Local Network Features

If you enable remote or output services, StageFlo can expose local network endpoints to devices you authorize on your network. You are responsible for controlling access to your local network and device.

Third-Party Components

StageFlo uses third-party libraries and platform services to provide core functionality, including packaging, media, and update workflows. These components are used to operate the app and are not used to sell personal data.

Data Retention and Control

Most StageFlo content is retained locally until you modify or delete it. You can remove data by deleting your local app data and project content.

Children's Privacy

StageFlo is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for church and ministry presentation workflows.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
